# ZEST / Launch content and experimentation plan
Self-initiated fictional snack concept. Proposed plan, not a campaign run.

## Objective
Build recognizable creative around the occasion of a mid-afternoon break. The business hypothesis is that a familiar occasion helps a new brand earn attention; no performance outcome is claimed.

## Storyboard / delivered silent reel
0–3 seconds: 3 PM. ZERO SPARK? Purple field; small zoom.
3–6 seconds: BREAK THE BORING. Yellow field; repeated pack silhouette.
6–9 seconds: YOUR NEXT BREAK = ZEST. Coral field; discovery message.
Output: H.264 MP4, 576 × 720 (4:5), 30 fps, 9 seconds; silent.
Feed source: 1080 × 1350. Story source: 1080 × 1920.

## A/B test proposal
Compare “3 PM. Zero spark?” with “Desk break?” while holding placement, audience, CTA, landing page, bid settings and format constant. Use a randomized platform split if available; avoid treating two different posting times as a controlled experiment.
Primary creative measure: link clicks / impressions (CTR).
Guardrails: landing-page engagement and purchases / clicks; monitor cost per purchase when enough conversion data exists. A high CTR alone does not establish business success.
Determine sample size from baseline rate, minimum meaningful lift and chosen power before launch. Do not declare a winner from a few clicks. Record exclusions and stop rules before running.

## Review and handoff
Day 1: align brief and audience assumptions with brand owner.
Days 2–3: concept review, consolidate feedback, approve direction.
Days 4–5: finish assets, review platform overlays and copy, export and hand off.
Track owner, format, due date, approval status, version and placement for each asset.

## Boundaries
No actual consumer research, live campaign, licensed music, filmed footage, product nutrition claims or platform performance is represented. Product packaging is illustrative, not food-label-compliant production artwork.
